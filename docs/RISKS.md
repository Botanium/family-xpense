# Family Xpense: Risks

Likelihood and impact are High, Medium or Low. "Tripwire" is the observation that tells us the risk has become real. Work package ids refer to [DELEGATION.md](DELEGATION.md).

## Top five

1. **R-03 Kurdish speech-to-text** may not work well enough on any engine.
2. **R-02 A2UI packages are pre-1.0** and the server and phone are already on different core versions.
3. **R-05 On-device models** do not cover Arabic or Kurdish on most phones.
4. **R-06 Decisions API** is beta, with no stated language coverage.
5. **R-13 Partner privacy** depends on a denormalised field staying correct.

- **TypeScript versus Dart backend:** the JS A2UI plugin is still on v0.9, so the Dart decision holds.
- **`liquid_glass_renderer`:** ruled out; no evaluation needed.
